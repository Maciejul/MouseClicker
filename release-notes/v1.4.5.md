## Changes in 1.4.5
- The Hold trigger on/off control is now a wide switch at the top of the Hold tab. It is grey and reads OFF when the trigger is disabled, and green and reads ON when it is enabled.
- The Start / Stop button label is white, so it is easy to read on the blue and green button.

See [v1.4.2](https://github.com/Maciejul/MouseClicker/releases/tag/v1.4.2) for the full list of 1.4 features: timed holds, mouse button holds, click options, auto-stop, profiles and startup settings.
