## Changes in 1.4.8
- On the Hold tab, "Key and mouse button" puts the mouse button list beside the key to hold, so the two things you hold sit on one row.
- With "Mouse button" only, the key to hold is hidden, since no key is held. The mouse button list stays under What to hold.

See [v1.4.2](https://github.com/Maciejul/MouseClicker/releases/tag/v1.4.2) for the full list of 1.4 features: timed holds, mouse button holds, click options, auto-stop, profiles and startup settings.
