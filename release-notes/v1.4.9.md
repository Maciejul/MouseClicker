## Changes in 1.4.9
- On the Hold tab, the key and the mouse button each have their own labelled control under the trigger key: **KEY TO HOLD** and **MOUSE KEY TO HOLD**.
- "Keyboard key" shows only the key, "Mouse button" shows only the mouse button, and "Key and mouse button" shows both.

See [v1.4.2](https://github.com/Maciejul/MouseClicker/releases/tag/v1.4.2) for the full list of 1.4 features: timed holds, mouse button holds, click options, auto-stop, profiles and startup settings.
