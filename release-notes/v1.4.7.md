## Changes in 1.4.7
- The Hold tab only shows the details of an option while that option is in use:
  - "Max while pressed" shows when a single tap holds while the key is pressed.
  - The double tap and triple tap times show when multiple taps hold for a set time is ticked.
  - "Time added" shows when tapping during a timed hold adds time.
- The Hold trigger switch reads ENABLED (green) or DISABLED (grey) instead of ON and OFF.
- The Hold trigger on/off control is a wide switch at the top of the Hold tab, and the Start / Stop button label is white.

See [v1.4.2](https://github.com/Maciejul/MouseClicker/releases/tag/v1.4.2) for the full list of 1.4 features: timed holds, mouse button holds, click options, auto-stop, profiles and startup settings.
