## What's new in the 1.4 series

### Hold
- Hold a keyboard key, a mouse button (left, right or middle), or both.
- Single tap: toggle on and off, hold while the trigger is pressed (with an optional maximum time), or do nothing.
- Timed hold: multiple taps hold for a set time. A double tap holds for 5 s and a triple tap for 10 s by default, and both are editable.
- Tapping during a timed hold can add time instead of stopping it.
- The time left shows in the status pill and on the Hold tab, and a message at the top of the window says when the hold is released.

### Clicker
- Left, right or middle button, with an optional double click.
- Click at the cursor, at a fixed screen point, or at a spot inside a window. The window is found again by process name, so the spot follows it if it moves.
- Auto-stop after a number of clicks or minutes.

### Settings (new tab)
- Profiles: save named sets of clicker and hold settings, and switch with a list or a key (default F8).
- Start with Windows, start minimized, and minimize to the tray.

### Fixes in 1.4.2
- Long option labels on the Hold tab wrap instead of being cut off.
- The jitter label shows the saved value at startup.
- The third tab is named Settings.
