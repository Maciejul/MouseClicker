## Changes in 1.4.6
- The Hold trigger switch on the Hold tab reads ENABLED (green) or DISABLED (grey) instead of ON and OFF.

Also new since 1.4.4:
- The Hold trigger on/off control is a wide switch at the top of the Hold tab.
- The Start / Stop button label is white.

See [v1.4.2](https://github.com/Maciejul/MouseClicker/releases/tag/v1.4.2) for the full list of 1.4 features: timed holds, mouse button holds, click options, auto-stop, profiles and startup settings.
